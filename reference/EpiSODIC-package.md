# EpiSODIC: Epidemiological Signal Observation, Detection, Identification, and Classification

An automated surveillance system for infectious disease epidemiologists
that detects aberrations in laboratory-confirmed infections, records
epidemiologist's formal assessment with a full audit trail, and produces
outbreak reports for clinical colleagues and infection prevention
nurses. Applies evidence-based statistical methods such as Improved
Farrington (2013)
[doi:10.1002/sim.5595](https://doi.org/10.1002/sim.5595) and 'EpiEstim'
(Cori et al. 2013)
[doi:10.1093/aje/kwt133](https://doi.org/10.1093/aje/kwt133) . Ships
with a synthetic demo data set requiring no external data or
infrastructure.
